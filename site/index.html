More Than Just A Social Game Night

Created by Robert Kiyosaki, author of Rich Dad Poor Dad, Cashflow is a financial simulation about one goal: Getting out of the rat race.

To get there, you’ll manage income, expenses, debt, opportunities, and unexpected life events, just like in real life.

You’ll also see why property, when chosen and managed well, can be one of the most powerful assets in a wealth-building strategy.

It sounds simple. It’s not. But that’s what makes the learning so fun.
