What if you could test years of financial decisions in one evening?

More Than Just A Social Game Night

More Than Just A Social Game Night

Created by Robert Kiyosaki, author of Rich Dad Poor Dad, Cashflow is a financial simulation about one goal: Getting out of the rat race.

To get there, you’ll manage income, expenses, debt, opportunities, and unexpected life events, just like in real life.

You’ll also discover why property, when chosen and managed well, can be one of the most powerful assets in a wealth-building strategy.

▪ Around the table

But you won’t be playing alone.

You’ll be making deals, negotiating, comparing decisions and watching how other investors respond to the same opportunities you face.

Sometimes, you learn just as much from the person across the table as you do from the game itself.

01What is the Investor Round Table (Cashflow Game) session about?

This is more than just playing a board game.

Throughout the session, Ivan and Benny will guide the gameplay, break down key decisions, and connect what happens in the game to real-life cashflow, investing and wealth-building situations.

The goal is not simply to win — it’s to understand how cash flow works and apply it to real life.

02Do I need investment experience to join?

No. The session is suitable for both beginners and experienced investors.

You’ll explore practical concepts such as cashflow, assets, liabilities, leverage, risk and opportunity in an interactive and easy-to-understand environment.

03Will I get to interact with other investors?

Yes. That’s part of the Investor Round Table experience.

You’ll play, discuss, negotiate and make decisions together with other participants — learning not only from Ivan and Benny, but also from the different perspectives around your table.

04How much is the commitment fee?

The commitment fee is $15 for 1 pax and $25 for 2 pax. If you’re bringing a plus one, please register and make payment for your partner as well.

The fee helps us secure your seat and prepare the session accordingly. All payments are non-refundable. Light refreshments will be provided.
